Program variant
Time budget. Each full day carries 330 instructional minutes (5.5 hours of activities and instruction). Non-instructional time sits on top — 2 × 30-min breaks, 60 min lunch, 30 min buffer = 150 min — for an 8-hour elapsed day. The wrap-up day is 240 minutes (4.0 hours, lunch during discussion). The 30-minute buffer is held as day-level slack and is deliberately not distributed into activity durations.
