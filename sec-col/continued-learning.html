Continued Learning

Ongoing

A list and description of the various professional learning and training I have undertaken over the past 10+ years. Webpages to training and earned certificates are linked. Also see my CV and LinkedIn profile.

Overview

Learning is an on-going process and truly life-long. I have learned countless lessons and ways to succeesfully accomplish objectives through my work experience. But through continued learning and training I have been able to draw connections between lessons and ultimately bring more value into my work experiences, as well as prepare for things yet to come.

In general, I will take on training for two reasons: 1) to improve my knowledge and skills in support of my current work, or 2) to improve my existing knowledge and skills for what I see ahead. But occassionally, these happen along with doing training for fun. I have often found that if it feels fun, then it doesn't feel like work and I take more from it. Not surprisingly, this is the kind of thinking I often take into work where I'm designing learning experiences for others.

Professional Learning and Training

Below I highlight the continued learning and training I've undertaken and completed. My endeavors fall into 3 categories:

  • AI, Information Technology, and Data
  • Business and Team Management
  • Instructional/Learning Design and Design Thinking

Additionally, this learning has been completed across various platforms such as edX, Coursera, and through other platforms or in-person workshops. The only experiences that I do not list are those that I took internal to organizations on topics of negotiation and leadership.
